Who we are

Doubloon: Coin Merge Puzzle ("the app") is developed by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app on Android. Contact: dco.dev.support@gmail.com.

Data we do not collect

We do not run our own servers and we do not ask for your name, e-mail address, location, contacts or photos. Your game progress (scores, virtual "wealth", settings, achievements, daily streak) is stored only on your device. The in-game currencies and wealth are virtual points with no real-world monetary value.

Advertising and your choices

Ads may be personalized based on your Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads). Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. Purchasing "Remove Ads" removes banner ads; rewarded ads remain optional.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app shows a local daily reminder. It is scheduled on your device; no data is sent to us. You can turn it off in the app or in Android settings.

Sharing

When you choose to share a score or a challenge code, the app opens Android's share sheet. What is shared and with whom is entirely your choice.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all progress stored on your device. Leaderboard scores are stored by Google Play Games; you can manage or delete your Play Games data from your Google account. For any other request, contact us at the address above.

Security

Data exchanged with Google services is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.

Reklamlar reklam kimliğinize göre kişiselleştirilebilir. Reklam kimliğinizi cihaz ayarlarından sıfırlayabilir, silebilir veya kişiselleştirilmiş reklamları kapatabilirsiniz. Bildirimler
